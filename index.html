Classes & schedule

Find your class

Filter by level — each day lists pilates and yoga together. Intermediate and Expert open with both pilates styles.

Beginner · Gentle

Flow & Align

A mindful, low-impact class designed to reconnect you with your body. Expect slow, controlled movements that improve posture, build core awareness, and leave you feeling grounded and aligned. Perfect for beginners or anyone looking for a restorative flow.

Intermediate

Tone & Sculpt

A dynamic mat pilates class designed to build strength, define your muscles, and improve endurance. Expect a mix of controlled movements and challenging sequences that target your core, glutes, and overall body tone — leaving you stronger, sculpted, and energized after every session.

Intermediate

Strengthen & Stretch

A balanced flow that works both strength and flexibility. You’ll engage your core and challenge your muscles while also opening up tight areas like hips, hamstrings, and shoulders. Expect to walk out taller, looser, and stronger.

Expert · High energy

Power Core

Designed for those ready to push their limits, this class combines intense core drills, stability work, and flowing transitions. It builds stamina, carves definition, and leaves you sweating — a full-body challenge with pilates precision.

Expert · High energy

Dynamic Flow

An upbeat, fast-paced sequence where one movement flows into the next. You’ll work your entire body through dynamic transitions that improve strength, control, and endurance. Ideal for clients who love a challenge and want to leave feeling powerful and energized.
